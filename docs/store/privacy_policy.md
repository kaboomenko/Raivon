## What we collect
| Data | Why | Kept |
|---|---|---|
| A random device/install identifier (guest account) | to keep your game progress and restore it | while the account exists |

## Children
The game is rated 16+. Players who state they are under 13 do not receive personalized ads and cannot use free-text chat.

## Third parties
Google Play Services / Apple App Store (purchases), RevenueCat (purchase validation), AppLovin MAX and Google AdMob (ads), Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications), our server host (Hetzner, EU). Each processes data under its own policy.

## Your rights
You can request a copy of your data or delete your account in the game settings or by e-mail to [support e-mail]. Account deletion removes your progress within 30 days (purchases records may be kept as required by law). EU/EEA users have the rights under GDPR; you can also complain to your data protection authority.

## Changes
We will update this page and the date above when the policy changes.
